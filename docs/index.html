Co-SDR: A Cochlear-Domain SDR Loss for Biophysically-Informed Speech Enhancement

François Effa*, Romain Serizel

Audio Samples

Audio samples complementing the experimental results reported in the paper [1]. They illustrate the perceptual differences between speech enhancement systems trained with standard SI-SDR and the proposed cochlear-domain Co-SDR loss. A PyTorch implementation of the CoNNear model (with pretrained weights) and the associated Co-SDR loss function, is available at GitHub repository.

The audio samples correspond to the same utterance presented in broadband stationary speech-shaped noise in a reverberant room, with the room acoustics reproduced by convolution with a measured room impulse response (RIR) from the BinauRec dataset [2]. The SNR values refer to the signal-to-noise ratio measured at the microphone input during speech-active intervals.
